Custom icon

Your icon fills the top-left corner of the header for everyone. In the self-hosted version it's also shown on the sign-in screen and used as the app's icon for browser tabs, bookmarks and "Add to Home Screen". It's resized and cropped automatically: square to wide images fill the space; taller or extra-wide ones are trimmed from the center. PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP.

Ping check

Checks whether each device answers a ping and shows the result as a circle on every intersection bar: all devices reply · some don't · none reply · check off or nothing to check.

Pings are sent by the server of the self-hosted (Docker) version — a web page can't ping network devices, so here every circle stays grey.

Backup & Restore

A backup holds all the app's data: intersections, devices, address pools, the Device list, roles & privileges, custom device fields and the custom icon (user accounts and passwords aren't included and aren't changed by a restore). Backups work in both the claude.ai and self-hosted versions.
